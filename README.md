Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Word for the Boss](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-badger-44-one-word-for-the-boss-6b48c735): exactly one word describing your boss.

The word is **"Looming"** — every boss looms: over your shoulder, over the deadline, over the meeting that could have been an email.

`main.py` (pure Python standard library) writes that single word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. No network, no dependencies, deterministic output — run it twice and the entry is byte-identical.
